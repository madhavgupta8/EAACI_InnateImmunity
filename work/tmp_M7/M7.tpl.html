1. The master map

Read the map from top to bottom. Each stage has a typical disease when it fails. The rule from M6 applies to all stages: the lost function predicts the disease.

3. Classic exam traps

  1. TLR3 is the only TLR that does not use MyD88. TLR4 is on the surface but uses both MyD88 and TRIF. [[M3-F025]]
  2. Pyroptosis affects macrophages and DCs, not neutrophils. [[M3-F059]]
  3. Weak NOD2 variants increase Crohn's disease risk; NOD2 gain of function causes Blau syndrome. [[M3-F048,M3-F049]]
  4. Tryptase, not histamine, is the marker of mast cell activation. [[M2-F077]]
  5. Eosinophils have low FcεRI without a signalling chain. There is no convincing evidence that IgE activates them. [[M2-F090]]
  6. Lysozyme works better against Gram-positive bacteria. LPS covers the peptidoglycan of Gram-negative bacteria. [[M1-F040]]
  7. Anti-TNF drugs gave no benefit in sepsis trials and can reactivate tuberculosis. [[M1-F109,M1-F111]]
  8. In LAD, blood shows leukocytosis, but tissue has no pus. [[M4-F086,M4-F084]]
  9. NADPH oxidase makes superoxide. MPO makes hypohalous acid from H₂O₂. [[M4-F051,M4-F052]]
  10. For C2, the larger bound fragment is C2a. [[M5-F005]]
  11. Free IgG or IgM does not activate C1; IgG4 does not activate complement. [[M5-F017,M5-F015]]
  12. Factor H regulates only the alternative pathway. C4BP regulates C4b. [[M5-F074,M5-F076]]
  13. CD59 blocks C9 at C5b-8; DAF dissociates the C3 convertases. [[M5-F083,M5-F081]]
  14. Normal C1-INH does not exclude HAE (HAE nC1-INH). [[M6-F042]]
  15. Wheals do not exclude HAE, because chronic spontaneous urticaria can coexist. Erythema marginatum is often mistaken for urticaria. [[M6-F067,M6-F070]]
  16. C4 is not useful for HAE diagnosis below age 12 months. [[M6-F072]]
  17. PNH is not inherited. The PIG-A mutation is somatic. [[M6-F032]]

5 key points

  1. Innate immunity uses germline-encoded, nonclonal receptors and has limited memory. [[M1-F017,M1-F024,M1-F008]]
  2. Location predicts the ligand: surface TLRs see microbial walls; endosomal TLRs see nucleic acids. [[M3-F009,M3-F010]]
  3. The lost function predicts the disease: early classical → SLE; C3 → pyogenic; C5–C9 → Neisseria. [[M6-F002,M6-F008,M6-F017]]
  4. HAE-1/2 is bradykinin-mediated. Test C1-INH function, C1-INH protein and C4; use C1q for acquired deficiency. [[M6-F052,M6-F059,M6-F064]]
  5. Innate immunity gives signal 2 (costimulators, cytokines, complement products) and starts adaptive immunity. [[M1-F112,M1-F114]]

3 exam traps

  1. TLR3 is the only TLR without MyD88; TLR4 uses MyD88 and TRIF. [[M3-F025]]
  2. C3 and CH50 are normal in HAE; normal C1-INH does not exclude HAE. [[M6-F061,M6-F042]]
  3. LAD gives leukocytosis in blood but no pus in tissue. Do not confuse it with neutropenia. [[M4-F086,M4-F084]]
